Submission & AI Classification Pipeline

1
Citizen submits complaint
POST /api/complaints/ — title, description, category, lat/lng, ward_id. JWT required.
2
AI Classification
Sends prompt to ibm/granite-13b-instruct-v2 → returns category, priority, confidence, summary. If WATSONX_API_KEY is unset, rule-based keyword classifier runs (confidence = keyword_matches / 3.0, capped at 1.0).
3
SLA Deadline set
Based on AI-assigned priority: Critical=4h · High=24h · Medium=72h · Low=168h. Stored as sla_deadline.
4
Auto-assign Contractor
Highest compliance_score contractor in the ward auto-selected. total_assigned += 1 immediately.
5
Officers act — status transitions
PATCH /api/complaints/{id} — set status, priority, assignee_id, contractor_id. Each change logs a ComplaintUpdate audit record with timestamp and note.
6
Resolution & Compliance Score Update
On status=resolved: resolved_at stamped. compliance_score = round(total_resolved/total_assigned × 100, 1)
7
Citizen rates satisfaction
POST /api/complaints/{id}/rate — integer 1–5. Only the reporter can rate. Stored as citizen_rating.
Escalation Matrix
How complaints move up the authority chain when SLA is at risk or breached

Escalation Levels & Path

🟢 Normal — Open
Citizen submits → AI classifies → contractor auto-assigned → status: OPEN
Who acts: Field Officer
🟡 Level 1 — SLA at 50% elapsed
Complaint still not in_progress at halfway. Ward Officer should intervene, re-assign or raise priority.
Who acts: Ward Officer — re-assign field team
🟠 Level 2 — SLA Breach Imminent
Not resolved before sla_deadline. Ward Officer sets status → ESCALATED. Commissioner sees it in dashboard.
Who acts: Ward Officer → Commissioner review
🔴 Level 3 — SLA Breached
Deadline passed. Contractor compliance_score auto-recalculated on eventual resolution. Commissioner reassigns contractor or ward officer, or force-closes.
Who acts: Commissioner — contractor penalised

Priority → SLA Window

CRITICAL
4h emergency/flood/overflow HIGH
24h dangerous/hazard MEDIUM
72h default LOW
168h minor/small

Contractor Compliance Impact

On every resolution:
compliance_score = (total_resolved / total_assigned) × 100

Auto-assignment always picks the contractor with the highest compliance_score in the ward.

SLA breaches put the contractor under Commissioner review → may lead to ward reassignment.

API Reference
All endpoints — Swagger UI at http://localhost:8000/docs

Auth & Wards

POST/api/auth/registerPublic
Register. Body: name, email, password, role, ward_id → returns UserOut (201)
POST/api/auth/loginPublic
Login. Body: email, password → access_token (JWT HS256 · 24h), token_type, user
GET/api/auth/meJWT
Returns current user profile
GET/api/wards/Public
All wards: id, name, zone, flood_risk_score, cleanliness_score
GET/api/wards/{id}/statsPublic
Ward stats: open_count, resolved_count, scores
GET/healthPublic
Returns {"status":"ok","service":"NagaraAI"}

Analytics

GET/api/analytics/cityJWT
City KPIs: total/open complaints, resolved_today, avg_resolution_hours, top_category, high_risk_wards (flood_risk ≥ 7.0)
GET/api/analytics/ward-heatmapJWT
Per-ward open count + flood_risk_score + cleanliness_score
GET/api/analytics/contractorsJWT
Leaderboard sorted by compliance_score desc, with resolution_rate

Complaints

POST/api/complaints/JWT
Submit complaint. AI classifies → SLA set → contractor auto-assigned. Returns full ComplaintOut (201).
GET/api/complaints/JWT
List. Role-scoped (citizen=own, field=assigned, ward=ward, commissioner=all). Params: status, category, ward_id, limit(50), offset(0)
GET/api/complaints/{id}JWT
Single complaint with full ComplaintUpdate audit history
PATCH/api/complaints/{id}JWT
Update: status, priority, assignee_id, contractor_id, note. Logs ComplaintUpdate row. On resolved: stamps resolved_at, recalculates compliance_score.
POST/api/complaints/{id}/rateJWT
Citizen rates 1–5. Only reporter can rate their own complaint. 400 if out of range.
ComplaintCreate body: title, description?, category, latitude?, longitude?, address?, image_url?, ward_id?
ComplaintUpdate body: status?, priority?, assignee_id?, contractor_id?, note?
User Guide
Step-by-step instructions for each role

👤 Citizen

1

Log in

Open http://localhost:3000. Click 👤 Citizen quick-fill button, then Sign In. Password: demo1234

2

Report a problem

Click + Report Issue. Fill in title and description — AI auto-classifies category and sets priority. Add location if known.

3

Track your reports

Click My Reports to see all submitted complaints, their status and SLA deadline countdown.

4

Rate the resolution

When a complaint shows RESOLVED, click Rate and choose 1–5 stars. This feeds contractor compliance scores.

5

Browse Ward Map

Click Ward Map to see active issues pinned on the Bengaluru map.

🔧 Field Officer

1

Log in

Use 🔧 Field Officer quick-fill. You'll land on My Tasks — complaints assigned specifically to you.

2

Start working

Open a complaint → change status to IN PROGRESS + add a note describing the action.

3

Mark resolved

Once work is complete → status RESOLVED. This stamps resolved_at and updates the contractor's compliance score.

4

Watch SLA

sla_deadline is shown per ticket. Resolve before it to avoid escalation to Ward Officer.

🏛️ Ward Officer

1

Log in

Use 🏛️ Ward Officer. You see Ward Issues — all complaints in your ward.

2

Assign field teams

Open complaint → set assignee_id to a field officer. Status auto-moves to ASSIGNED.

3

Escalate SLA risks

If a complaint approaches breach and unresolved → status to ESCALATED + note for Commissioner.

4

Monitor analytics

Use Dashboard and Ward Map to spot blackspots and check contractor performance in your ward.

📊 Commissioner

1

Log in

Use 📊 Commissioner. The City Dashboard shows real-time city-wide KPIs.

2

Monitor city health

City stats: total/open complaints, resolved today, avg resolution hours, top category, wards with flood_risk ≥ 7.0.

3

Review escalations

Filter All Issues by status=escalated. Reassign contractors or ward officers. Force-close stale tickets.

4

Contractor leaderboard

Identify underperforming contractors (low compliance_score). Reassign their ward to higher-performing contractors.

5

Ward Heatmap

Per-ward open complaint count + risk scores. High flood_risk_score wards need proactive drain and waterlogging monitoring before monsoon.
